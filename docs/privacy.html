Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

Flourish BX is a pickup ordering app for our restaurant at 4035 Laconia Ave. We ask for the least we can: your name so we know whose order to call out, and your phone number so staff can reach you about an order. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone.

What we collect

✓ We collect

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your order history
  • Your loyalty points balance

× We never collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Your photos
  • Health or fitness data
  • Browsing or search history from outside this app
  • Any advertising identifier

Why we collect it

Each piece of information has one job:

  • Name — so staff can match the bag to the person at the counter.
  • Phone number — so staff can reach you about an order, and so we can find your points when you order again.
  • Order history — so you can reorder your usual in one tap.
  • Loyalty points — so you get credit for what you have spent with us.

We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or tracking you across other apps and websites.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell your personal information, we do not rent it, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or any other third party. The only place your order goes is our own kitchen register.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Clover, our point-of-sale provider, which is PCI-DSS compliant. When you pay by card in the app, your card details are entered directly into a secure form hosted by Clover and are sent straight to them.

This app never sees, handles or stores your card number. We receive only a confirmation that the payment succeeded. You can also choose to pay at the counter when you collect your order, in which case no card details are entered in the app at all.

Where your information is kept

Your name, phone number, order history and points are stored on your own device so the app works when you open it, and in our Clover point-of-sale account so staff can look up your order and your points at the counter. We keep them for as long as you have an account with us.

Deleting your account

You can have your account and all of the information above deleted at any time. Call the restaurant on (347) 859-9413 or ask at the counter, and tell us the phone number on the account. We will delete it and confirm.

You can also clear everything stored on your own device by deleting the app.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app can notify you when your order is ready. That notification is scheduled on your own device — nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and no notification token or device identifier is collected. Declining costs you nothing: the order status is on screen the whole time either way, and you can change your mind in your phone’s settings.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Children

Flourish BX is a food ordering app for the general public. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, call us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Meaningful changes will also be announced in the app.
